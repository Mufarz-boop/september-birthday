For you.

Today is September 14. For most people, it may be just another day on the calendar — a Tuesday, unremarkable, slipping quietly past without notice.

But today is the day someone important to this little story was born. And I think that deserves a moment of pause.

I've always believed that birthdays are strange and wonderful things. They arrive whether we're ready or not, carrying with them a quiet reminder that time is moving, that we are growing, that we are still here. Still breathing. Still becoming.

So today, I hope you take a breath. I hope you notice the small things — the warmth of your coffee, the light through the window, the sound of someone laughing nearby. I hope you let yourself be proud of how far you've come, even on the days it doesn't feel like much.

You don't have to be extraordinary today. You just have to be here. That's enough. That's always been enough.

And if this year has been heavy, I hope this next one is lighter. If it's been good, I hope it gets even better. Either way, I hope you keep going — gently, bravely, at your own pace.
